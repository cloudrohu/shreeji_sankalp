Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

Find answers to some of the most common questions about Vinayak Group and our residential projects.

Who is Vinayak Group?
Vinayak Group is a real estate developer focused on creating thoughtfully planned residential spaces with an emphasis on quality, design, connectivity and customer satisfaction.
Where are Vinayak Group projects located?
Our projects are focused on strategically selected locations in and around Gandhinagar, Gujarat, with convenient connectivity to major destinations.
What type of properties does Vinayak Group develop?
Vinayak Group develops residential properties designed to offer modern layouts, comfortable living spaces, lifestyle amenities and convenient connectivity.
How can I enquire about a project?
You can contact our team through the enquiry form, phone number or contact details provided on the website. Our team can help you with project details, availability and site visits.
Can I schedule a site visit?
Yes. Contact our team to discuss your preferred date and time, and our representatives can assist with arranging a project or site visit.
Where can I find RERA and legal information?
Project-specific RERA registration details and applicable legal information can be provided for the respective project. Please contact our team for the relevant documentation.
